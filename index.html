About

Research-oriented personal website for academic and technical work.

I am Usman Nawaz, a PhD student at the University of Palermo (UNIPA). My work focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP) and applied AI, with interests in language technologies and computational methods for complex textual data.

My current PhD research includes AI techniques for the analysis of ancient languages, combining NLP methods with linguistically informed approaches.
